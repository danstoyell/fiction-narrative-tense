# Conflict narration reviews

These 20 reports use the project rule: label the **base narration**, not the
surface tense of aphorisms, dialogue, memories, or a short frame. `CONFLICT`
means the structural `narrating_situation` and the pooled event/dialogue-beat
tense ratio disagree. The 14 current production conflicts plus six early-period
records recomputed with `analyze_year.py`'s same rule reconcile the total.

No production labels are changed by this review. Reports marked **CHANGE** are
recommended corrections for a subsequent, deliberate relabel pass.
