# South of Broad — Pat Conroy

**Conclusion: REAFFIRMS `PAST`.** Leo's reflective present-tense observations
sit inside a retrospective, nonlinear account of his life.

- **Current audit:** `retrospective`; 15 past vs. 10 present event quotations,
  with no dialogue-beat evidence. The present items are generalized reflection
  and description, while the event material predominantly recounts earlier
  experience.
- **External check:** contemporary reviews identify Leo King as the narrator,
  locate a framing strand in 1990, and describe the book's returns to 1969 and
  later decades. [Kirkus review](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/pat-conroy/south-of-broad/), [Malcolm's Round Table review](https://malcolmsroundtable.com/2009/09/27/book-review-pat-conroys-south-of-broad/).
- **Action:** retain `PAST` / `retrospective`; do not elevate the reflective
  present to a co-equal base narrative.
