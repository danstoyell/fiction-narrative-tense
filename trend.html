What this does and doesn't support

It is consistent with a rise, and it is not yet evidence of one. 2016–2019 average about 6% present across 46 labelled books; 2020–2025 average about 30% across 57. That gap is large. But every year's confidence interval overlaps several others, and the two halves differ in ways that have nothing to do with tense.

  • Books within a year are not a random subset. Processing order was mid-size-first, then round-robin across the thinnest-covered years. So a year at n=6 is showing its ~35-quote books, while a year at n=18 includes both thinner and richer ones. Quote count correlates with how much event-narration a book yields, and possibly with genre.
  • 2019 sits at 0% on n=7 — the only year with no present-tense book at all. With 7 books, that is entirely compatible with the same underlying rate as 2018 or 2020.
  • Abstentions are excluded from the denominator, and they are not random. Dialogue-heavy commercial fiction abstains most: two Yarros novels returned 3 and 4 event-narration quotes against 19 and 16 dialogue quotes, and both read as present-tense. If that genre skews present and abstains more, this chart understates present tense.
  • Dual is a finding, not a failure. Books like Golden Girl and Happy Place alternate tense structurally; they are counted separately rather than forced into either band.
